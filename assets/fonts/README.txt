FONT FILES REQUIRED FOR PDF GENERATION
======================================

Download Roboto font files from Google Fonts and place them here:
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto

Required files:
- Roboto-Regular.ttf
- Roboto-Bold.ttf
- Roboto-Italic.ttf
- Roboto-BoldItalic.ttf

Or download from:
https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto/releases
